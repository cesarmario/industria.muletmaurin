Our History and Origins

We are a family-owned agroindustrial company deeply rooted in the Province of San Juan, Argentina. Our journey began in 1980 with the cultivation of grapevines on land that has belonged to the family for over a century. With the firm purpose of establishing ourselves as quality producers, in 1991 we founded our winery.
 
True to a philosophy of continuous growth and diversification, in 2021 we inaugurated our raisin processing plant, achieving the vertical integration of every stage of production — from the field to the final product.

THE WINERY

Our winery has a capacity of 15 million liters and receives 15,000 tons of grapes annually. It features modern technology and machinery of French and Italian origin, combined with traditional aging in concrete tanks — a defining element of the high quality of our products.

GRAPE RAISIN PROCESSING PLANT

We sun-dry our grapes using a 100% natural process, taking full advantage of the exceptional agroclimatic conditions of San Juan province. The result is a consistent, top-quality raisin with naturally concentrated sweetness, highly valued in global markets. All raw materials come exclusively from our own crops, ensuring complete traceability from vine to final product. We operate a state-of-the-art processing, sorting, calibrating, and packaging line.

AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTION

We own 400 hectares of vineyards and crops in continuous expansion, strategically distributed across locations selected for their ideal soil and microclimate — ensuring the finest expression of our raw materials.
 
ORIGIN GUARANTEE Our fully integrated agricultural and industrial model allows us to certify complete traceability, guaranteeing a reliable, safe, and consistent supply year-round for our global distributors.
 
 
 

SOIL ENGINEERING SERVICES

To achieve maximum operational efficiency, the company owns and operates its own heavy machinery — including large tractors, backhoes, grading blades, and bulldozers — providing agricultural and civil engineering services such as land leveling, brush clearing, canal construction, drainage systems, and subsoiling, both for internal use and for third parties.

Location

San Juan is located in the central-western region of Argentina, at the foot of the Andes mountain range — one of the key factors that makes it an outstanding wine-growing region. Its semi-arid climate, with only 200 mm of annual rainfall concentrated in summer, requires irrigation carried out with meltwater from the Andes.
